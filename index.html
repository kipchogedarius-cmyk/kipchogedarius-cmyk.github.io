About Us

Cheptarit Vocational Training Center is a public Vocational Training Centre (VTC) registered and licensed by the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority (TVETA). Registration No: TVETA/PUBLIC/VTC/0157/2016 (Registered 19 September 2016).

It is located along the Eldoret–Kapsabet Road, next to the DO’s Office, Mosoriot, Nandi County. The centre offers practical Artisan and Trade Test courses examined by NITA and KNEC to equip youth with marketable skills.

Courses Offered

  • Trade Test in Motor Vehicle Mechanics (NITA) – max 40 trainees
  • Trade Test in Masonry (NITA) – max 30 trainees
  • Trade Test in Electrical Installation (NITA) – max 20 trainees
  • Trade Test in Dress Making (NITA) – max 30 trainees
  • NVCET in Fashion Design & Garment Making Technology (KNEC) – max 30 trainees
  • Trade Test in Carpentry and Joinery (NITA) – max 20 trainees
  • Artisan in Food and Beverage (KNEC) – max 20 trainees
  • Trade Test in Plumbing (NITA) – max 40 trainees
  • Trade Test in Welding and Fabrication (NITA) – max 20 trainees
  • Trade Test in Hairdressing (NITA) – max 30 trainees

Other related artisan courses (Computer Packages, Beauty Therapy, etc.) may also be available – confirm with the centre.
