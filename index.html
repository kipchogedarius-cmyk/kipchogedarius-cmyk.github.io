Cheptarit Vocational Training Center

A public TVETA-registered vocational college in Nandi County, offering artisan and trade test courses in motor vehicle mechanics, masonry, electrical installation, carpentry, plumbing, welding, fashion design, hairdressing, food and beverage, and more.

Fees

Fees are subsidized — the school is sponsored.

Boarding

KSh 32,000 per year

  • Term 1: KSh 18,500
  • Term 2: KSh 10,500
  • Term 3: KSh 3,000

Day Scholar

KSh 24,000 per year

  • Term 1: KSh 16,000
  • Term 2: KSh 6,500
  • Term 3: KSh 1,000
