Welcome to Cheptarit VTC

A public TVETA-registered vocational college in Nandi County offering artisan and trade test courses in Motor Vehicle Mechanics, Masonry, Electrical Installation, Carpentry, Plumbing, Welding, Fashion Design, Hairdressing, Food & Beverage, and more.

🎓

Subsidized Fees

The school is sponsored. Fees are highly subsidized for both boarding and day scholars.

🛠️

Practical Skills

Hands-on training in high-demand trades that prepare students for immediate employment.

📍

Easy Location

Located on Eldoret-Kapsabet Road, next to the DO's office, Mosoriot, Nandi County.
