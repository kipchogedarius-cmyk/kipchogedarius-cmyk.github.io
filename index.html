Assessment & Certification

Examinations

Students are assessed through practical work and examinations as applicable to their training programs.

1. Internal Assessments

Theory and practicals form part of training. Continuous Assessment Tests (CATs) are conducted at the school as internal examinations.

2. NITA Examinations

NITA stands for National Industrial Training Authority. These examinations assess relevant trade and practical skills.

3. National Examinations

The college information provided identifies NEC examinations as Kenya National Examination Council examinations. Please confirm the official examination body and level with the college.

Examination Schedule

External examinations are scheduled for July and November, as provided by the college. Registration is done through the school.

Our Institution

About Cheptarit VTC

Our School

Cheptarit Vocational Training Center provides vocational and practical skills training intended to prepare learners for work, self-employment, and further skills development.

The college is located along the Eldoret–Kapsabet Road in Mosoriot, Nandi County, Kenya.

Church Sponsorship

The school is described as being sponsored by St. Francis Chapel Catholic Parish under the Roman Catholic Diocese of Kapsabet.
